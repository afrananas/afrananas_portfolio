## Vision statement

Two minutes on the site should be enough for a visitor to learn what Afran builds, see evidence that it works, and find out how to make contact. Twenty minutes should be enough to understand how Afran thinks as an engineer.

## Positioning

The site is for recruiters and engineering teams hiring for backend and applied-AI work. It presents an engineer who can take a system from requirements to production and show evidence for each step.

Template portfolios list technologies and claim proficiency. This one documents real projects the way an engineering team documents a system: the problem, constraints, architecture, trade-offs, testing, measured results, and known limitations.

## Differentiation

| Typical developer portfolio | This portfolio |
|---|---|
| Skill bars and walls of logos | Each technology is named alongside what it was used for |
| Screenshots with a one-line description | Case studies covering architecture, decisions, and trade-offs |
| Claims without context, such as "improved performance by 80%" | Each result comes with its baseline, methodology, and a link to the evidence |
| Team projects presented as solo work | Each project states Afran's role and the scope of the contribution |
| Template design with heavy animation | A restrained, editorial, dark-first design that loads fast |
| Built once and never maintained | Built and run as a documented product |

## Principles that follow from the vision

1. **Evidence-led.** Every claim on the site can be verified. A claim that can't be verified is removed.
2. **Depth on demand.** Top-level pages can be scanned in seconds. Case studies give more detail to readers who want it.
3. **The site is a work sample.** Its performance, accessibility, code quality, and documentation are assessed along with everything else in the portfolio.
4. **Honest scope.** Academic work, research, and team contributions are labeled as exactly that.

[Design principles](../02-design/design-principles.md) turns these into visual and interaction rules.

## Long-term purpose

- **Near term:** support the current job search by making strong work easy to assess.
- **Ongoing:** give case studies and technical writing a permanent home as the career develops. The site should keep accumulating evidence rather than being rebuilt for each job search.
- **Side benefit:** the documentation also serves as a reference for building and running a small production system.

## Non-goals

- A blog platform, CMS, or social network.
- A showcase for visual effects.
- A complete archive of every project. The project list is curated.
